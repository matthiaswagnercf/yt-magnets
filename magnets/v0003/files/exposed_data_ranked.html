What this table cannot tell you

HIBP is a curated corpus of breaches that were loaded, not a census of every breach. "Passwords" counts hashed passwords too. The shift toward phones and addresses reflects what recent loaded breaches contained; it may also reflect which breaches get loaded. Washington State's public notice register (atg.wa.gov) shows the same pattern in its "Information Compromised" column for 2026 notices.
