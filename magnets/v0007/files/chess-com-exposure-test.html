Why HIBP calls a scrape a breach

Troy Hunt, who runs the database, wrote in 2021: "A data breach occurs when information is obtained by an unauthorised party in a fashion in which it was not intended to be made available." HIBP's API defines a breach as "an instance of a system having been compromised by an attacker and the data disclosed." Read the data classes; the word alone does not tell you whether a password moved.

NIST SP 800-63B, 5.1.1.2, on what a site should do with your new password: "verifiers SHALL compare the prospective secrets against a list that contains values known to be commonly-used, expected, or compromised," starting with "passwords obtained from previous breach corpuses." Most sites do not; step 2 does it for you.

What this cannot tell you

Whether your specific row carried an email address (about a quarter of rows did not). Whether Chess.com will say anything about the 2026 file; none of the sources read had a statement. The 2025 breach figures come from a report citing the Maine filing; the filing itself sits behind a login.
