If a tax letter arrives

  1. Do not call the number printed on it.
  2. Find the notice or letter code and look it up on irs.gov or canada.ca. No code is your answer.
  3. Call the agency's own number, from its site, and ask whether the notice exists.
  4. Threat plus deadline plus one phone number: report it (reportfraud.ftc.gov; in Canada, the Anti-Fraud Centre) and keep the page.

What this cannot tell you

The exhibit in the public filing is a scan and two lines are unreadable; the tells above use only lines the complaint quotes or that read clearly.
