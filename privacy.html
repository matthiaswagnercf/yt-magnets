Privacy Policy, Breach Record

Last updated: 2026-09-15

This page covers two things: the email list behind the free downloads on this site, and the personal software I use to publish videos to the Breach Record YouTube channel. The site is run by one person, Matthias Wagner, in Quebec, Canada.

What I collect. If you ask for a free download, I collect the email address you type in and, if you give it, your first name. Nothing else. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, and no third-party tracker on this page.

Why. To send you the file you asked for, and a short series of emails on the same topic. That is the only use.

Where it goes. Your address is stored in a private Google Sheet that only I can open, and it is transmitted through a small server function I run on Modal. Emails are sent through Gmail. I do not sell, rent, trade, or share your address with anyone, and I do not use it for advertising.

How long. Until you ask me to remove it. Reply to any email with "stop" or write to matthias.wagner.guitar@gmail.com and your address is deleted from the sheet, not just flagged. You can also ask me what I hold about you, or ask for a correction, and I will answer within 30 days.

Affiliate links. Some links in my videos, emails and pages are affiliate links: if you buy through one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Those links take you to a third party whose own cookies and privacy policy then apply. I have no access to anything you do on their site beyond a count of clicks and sales attributed to me.

YouTube. This channel is published with the help of a personal tool I wrote that uses YouTube API Services to upload my own videos and read my own channel's statistics. It never accesses your YouTube account or data. By using YouTube itself you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. If you have ever granted an application access to your own Google account, you can review and revoke it at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Children. Nothing here is directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 14.

Changes. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and anything material is said in an email to the list rather than quietly edited.

Contact / person responsible for personal information. Matthias Wagner, matthias.wagner.guitar@gmail.com.
